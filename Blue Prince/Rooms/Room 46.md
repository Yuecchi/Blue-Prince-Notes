## Description
The mysterious room mentioned in [[Herbert S. Sinclair]]'s Will, named only by a number. Despite the house's official blueprint only showing a total of 45 rooms, the words of the legal document makes it apparent that a 46th room in the house in fact exists. Finding this room is your primary objective while on the estate. It is after all, your _bequest_.
