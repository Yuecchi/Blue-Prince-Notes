## Description
After the passing of the [[Baroness Auravei]], her private study was claimed by her eldest son, Herbert Sinclair. For years, he immersed himself in the countless notebooks she left behind, drawing inspiration from her discarded ideas. From mechanical; puzzles to architectural blueprint, each endeavor he pursued in this room was realized in the shade of her creative legacy.
