## Description
A Utility room serving as a central hub for controlling heating and steam power supplied to various rooms of the estate. Steam power, Is conveyed in the estate through steam ducts that run through select rooms. These floorplans must be drafted in an alignment that will facilitate the steam traveling through the house to reach its intended destination.
