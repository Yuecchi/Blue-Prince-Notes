## Description
this facility, dedicated to the filtration and flow of water throughout the estate, houses five water pumps and three oversized tanks. The Pump Room can be utilized for a number of maintenance tasks, from draining the pool to activating the sprinkler system

Type: [[Mechanical]], [[Blueprint]], [[Addition]]
Rarity: [[Unusual]]

![[Pump Room - Pump Control]]

![[Pump Room - Schematic]]

Providing power from the [[Boiler Room]] provides access to the reserve tank. The reserve tank allows you to move water between itself and tank 1, and is required to fully drain [[The Pool]] and the the [[Resevoir]].
