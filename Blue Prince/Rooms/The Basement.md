Entry Locations Include:

- The Fountain (Must first be drained via the [[Pump Room]])
- The Resevoir
- The [[Foundation]] (At the bottom of the elevator shaft)

There is a side-room with a big red door on the way to the north lever.

The Door is locked and requires the [[The Basement Key]] to open it, which is located in the [[Antechamber]].
