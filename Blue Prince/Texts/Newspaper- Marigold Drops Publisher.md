[[Date]]: November 17
MARIGOLD DROPS PUBLISHER
Local author and popular personality, [[Marion Marigold]] has announced a split with her long term publishing partner, [[Ruffington Press]], citing personal reasons. The announcement came after the arrest last week of fellow author and Colleague, [[Caleb Manning]], whom [[Marigold]] has past referred to as her mentor, friend and confidant. [[Marigold]] is the third prominant writer to distance themselves from the Publisher this week.
