POOL SERIES 8
The EPCW PUMP SYSTEM is enginered specifically for the luxury requirements of today's indoor in-ground swimming pool and [[spa]]. The [[EPCW]] system is also expandable allowing you to use multiple pumps to control the water flow and volume to several different designated tanks and aquatic house features, be it an [[aquarium]], [[Fountain]] or good old-fashioned [[swimming pool]].

WATER CONTROL PANEL
Whether you're using a single tank system or an array of pumps, the WATER CONTROL PANEL servers as a central hub to your EPCW system. The panel is fitted with a number of buttons equal to your own home's needs: one button for each different water source on the estate.
The function is simple: Press a button to select the water source you would like to adjust. For example, if you wish to drain (or fill) your pool, simply press the button labeled "POOL".
Regardless of the number of pumps or water sources being used, water can only be adjusted from a source if its corresponding button is selected.
![[Pasted image 20261013105733.png]]

After you have selected a source using the WATER CONTROL PANEL, the next step is to set the pump associated with that source to pump either in or out. This will initiate water from to/from one of your supply tanks.
A pump may be connected to more than one tank allowing you to store and retrieve even more water (recommended for cleaning larger Olympic-size pools). 
In these cases, the tank a pump is using can be changed by a simple flip of a switch.
