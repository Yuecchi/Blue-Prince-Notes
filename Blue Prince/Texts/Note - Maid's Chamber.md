STAFF NOTICE

The day we all dreaded has finally arrived. It is with unprecedented sadness that we inform you of the death of our benefactor, employer and master, the Baron of Mount Holly, [H. S. Sinclair](Herbert%20S.%20Sinclair). Any staff who have an interest in attending services may contact [Mrs. Babbage](Anne%20Babbage) for details.

In following the instructions left by the Baron himself, effective immediately, all work at Mt. Holly is suspended. All live-in Staff will have one month paid leave and must vacate the premises by Friday, November 6th.

-[Mrs. Babbage](Anne%20Babbage)
