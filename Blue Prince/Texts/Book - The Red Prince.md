The Red Prince
by [[Marion Marigold]]
![[Pasted image 20261013111142.png]]
There was a red prince,
Who sat on a red throne.
He had a red crown made of rubies,
and a red castle made of stone.![[Pasted image 20261013111146.png]]

The prince had a love,
For all things red.
"It's the only _true_ color"
The prince often said.![[Pasted image 20261013111154.png]]

He could not stand the sight,
Of a canary or a crow.
He could not stand the white,
Of a swan in the snow.![[Pasted image 20261013111205.png]]

And he never looked up...
For fear of the view.
He had heard that the sky,
Was not a red hue!![[Pasted image 20261013111217.png]]
So he always had a book,
To turn his eyes down.
If he was ever outside,
When the clouds weren't around.![[Pasted image 20261013111223.png]]

And do you know which book,
The prince always had?
The one that made him happy,
Whenever he was sad?![[Pasted image 20261013111231.png]]

A story he had read
in the bath and the bed,
And maybe you can guess,
With the clues that we have said.![[Pasted image 20261013111235.png]]

It was the story of a boy,
Who also wore red!
And he looked like our prince,
From his toes to his head.![[Pasted image 20261013111244.png]]

And as you my reader,
Might already assume;
This book is quite near you,
In fact, in the same room!![[Pasted image 20261013111252.png]]

A story about you,
If it needs to be said.
About a red prince and his book.
The red book, we just read.![[Pasted image 20261013111258.png]]
