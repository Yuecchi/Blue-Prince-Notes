In the dim,
I see cavern walls of stone.
A hollow, lit by a sea of a thousand burning stars.
I see a treasure buried beneath the cliff rocks.
A black mirror, hidden among the memories of the falling sands
And within the black mirror, I see a figure.
A figure standing in sullen snow.
All of what I have told you my young baron, I wish you to now know
Until we meet again some distant day, I will have nothing more to say.
